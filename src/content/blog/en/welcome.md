Good ideas rarely arrive finished. They usually begin as a short note, a conversation, or a question worth keeping around a little longer.

This is a place to develop them: to share processes, references, and lessons while they happen.

## Starting with intention

This is not about publishing for the sake of it. Every article and project can be a window into a way of thinking and working.
